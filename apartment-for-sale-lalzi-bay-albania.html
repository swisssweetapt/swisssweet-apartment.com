The Location

+

The apartment sits toward the south of the residence, in its quieter, less built-up section — offering extra tranquility while remaining close to the shops, restaurants and the beach. Behind the building, and just south of the residence, the surrounding land is protected from further construction, preserving that calm. The apartment looks out over umbrella pines without sitting beneath them, allowing full sunlight through the day and warm sunsets from the balcony in the evening. Its through-apartment layout allows natural cross-ventilation, with mosquito screens on every opening so the air can circulate freely.

One parking space is included, though unassigned — residents park freely anywhere within the complex, with no restrictions. Traffic in the area is minimal, making it a safe, easy environment for children, dog walks, jogging or cycling.

Residents can also use San Pietro's on-site, five-star Meliá hotel: spa and fitness access can be arranged at a reduced rate through the apartment's management (standard entry is €35), while the restaurant and casino have no entrance fee — you simply pay for what you order. Beyond that, the hotel's rooftop bar is known for its sunset views over the bay, alongside several restaurants spanning Italian and Mediterranean cuisine.

San Pietro itself is run by an international resort operator to a five-star standard along Lalzit Bay's stretch of coastline — one of the more sought-after settings on this part of the Albanian Riviera. It sits about 23 km from central Durrës and roughly 28 km — around 35 to 40 minutes by car — from Tirana International Airport, with the capital itself about 40 minutes away. Close enough for a day in the city or a quick airport run, but far enough to leave the traffic, noise and density of Durrës or Tirana behind.

The Apartment

+

Kitchen

An open-plan, American-style kitchen, fully equipped with an oven, a washing machine and a full-size refrigerator, with generous storage throughout. A central island — combining closed storage with a raised table for four — separates the kitchen from the living area and adapts effortlessly from a morning coffee to dinner for four, or a shared home-office desk.

⊕

Living room

On the other side of the island, the living area centers on a comfortable 5-seat sofa, a coffee table and a TV unit with international channels. The apartment is wired for high-speed internet, comfortably supporting several people working from home at once. A sideboard adds further storage, and a reversible air conditioning unit keeps the room comfortable in every season.

⊕

Balcony

The balcony is built for outdoor living: a stone-clad built-in barbecue with enclosed storage beneath, a table seating six, and a hanging swing chair for slower afternoons. A wall cabinet discreetly houses the drying rack, cleaning supplies and the air conditioning condenser units — a level of finish above the norm in the residence, where these units are typically left exposed. A screened door with mosquito netting and a retractable awning complete the space.
